Eyvindarholt

We grew up next door

We grew up in Skógar, under the waterfall, and have spent forty years exploring this coast. In the autumn of 2019 we bought the old farm at Eyvindarholt, and with the help of friends we renovated it, so other people could enjoy this place the way we do.

Our seasons

Winter brings the northern lights over a peaceful farm. From April the birds come back from Europe and the fields fill up. In summer the highlands open, from Þórsmörk to Landmannalaugar.

  • WinterNorthern lights over the farm, seen from the door
  • AprilGeese, snipe and golden plover return to the fields
  • SummerÞórsmörk, Landmannalaugar and Hekla within reach

The DC-3

The plane that drove 716 km

A US Navy DC-3 first flew in 1944. On 25 July 1969 it ran off the gravel runway at Sauðanes in the far northeast, and all four aboard walked away. For decades it sheltered horses and sheep, until the farmers Ágúst and Steinunn gave it to Tómas, who wanted a viewing point on his land. It crossed Iceland on a trailer to face Stóri-Dímon.

How a stay works

  1. 1. Ask for your dates

    The request goes straight to Tómas, with no card and no charge. He confirms each stay personally.

  2. 2. Pick your roof

    The Hill House for ten, the Cabin for four, or Vallnatún for four. Groups take the Hill House and Cabin together.

  3. 3. Drive east along the coast

    Route 1 past Hvolsvöllur, where the last shops are, then five minutes on from Seljalandsfoss.

  4. 4. Let yourself in

    Self check-in at all three roofs. At the Hill House and the Cabin the key waits in a lockbox.

  5. 5. Day trips, if you want them

    Seljalandsfoss is five minutes, the Vestmannaeyjar ferry fifteen, Reynisfjara fifty. Þórsmörk and Landmannalaugar in summer.
